Scarp’s official project website brings the language’s published state, examples, releases, roadmap, and community links into one place.

## A project hub and a documentation home

The main site at **scarp-lang.github.io** explains what Scarp can do today and where it aims to go. [Scarp Docs](https://scarp-lang.github.io/scarp-docs/) remains the home for detailed guides and references in English and Hindi.

## Evidence beside ambition

The site uses a reviewed **v0.0.5-alpha.1** baseline. Implemented features appear separately from planned capabilities. Release information points to upstream records; future work carries no invented dates or progress percentages.

## Built in the open

The website uses Astro, TypeScript, and Markdown. Its source is [public and MIT licensed](https://github.com/scarp-lang/scarp-lang.github.io). Corrections to copy, accessibility, examples, and source evidence are welcome.
